# Crisis Mirror — Funding map (v0.1)

**What we’re funding:** camera-free smart-glasses **cognitive companion** for critical care  
(sim lab → education/QI → optional later bedside advisory)

**Not (yet):** an FDA-cleared autonomous diagnostic/alarm medical device company.

---

## Blunt take

| Path | Fit for Crisis Mirror now | Why |
|------|---------------------------|-----|
| **University / education / sim lab pots** | **Best first money** | Matches sim-first, advisory, camera-free story |
| **NIH / AHRQ research grants** | **Strong middle path** | CDS, safety, digital health validity — if you have academic PI + IRB home |
| **NIH SBIR/STTR** | **Strong if you form a small business** | Commercialization of digital health / CDS / workflow AI |
| **ARPA-H** | **Moonshot / later** | Big $$, high bar, program-fit dependent |
| **Foundations / society grants** | **Good glue money** | Smaller, faster, prestige for sim/education |
| **Hospital innovation / VC** | **After pilot data** | Need proof learners/clinicians want it on vs off |

**Do not lead with “we’re building a medical device.”**  
Lead with: **simulation education + cognitive forcing + safety-labeled AI cues**, camera-free, human remains decision-maker.

## Match product stage → money type

```
NOW (concept + sim)
  → internal Tx ICU / GME / sim-center funds
  → society education grants
  → small foundation / pilot awards

6–18 mo (working sim + labels + metrics)
  → NIH/AHRQ digital health / patient-safety R-series (with university)
  → NSF/NIH SBIR Phase I (if LLC/C-corp exists)

18–36 mo (multi-site sim or limited clinical shadow)
  → SBIR Phase II
  → larger AHRQ/PCORI-adjacent methods or implementation work
  → strategic partnership (glasses OEM, education vendor)

Only if you truly pursue regulated bedside product
  → FDA pathway budget + device-experienced partners
  → ARPA-H only if a live ISO/program fits
```

## 1) Fastest / lowest friction (start here)

### A. Your institution
| Source | What it buys | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| **Sim center / GME innovation** | Hardware (G2 or Halo cam-off), resident time, scenario build | Speak “learner cognitive forcing,” not “AI doctor” |
| **Dept / division pilot funds** | 1–2 glasses + phone app spike | Tx anesthesia / CC / surgery education budgets |
| **Hospital digital innovation / QI** | Pilot under QI umbrella | Camera-free helps PHI optics |

**Pitch one-liner:**  
*“Wearable HUD that whispers protocol checks in sim so fellows don’t freeze—then we measure if cues help.”*

### B. Specialty / education societies (examples to watch)
- ASA / SCA / SCCM / ASTS education or innovation awards  
- Simulation societies (SSH and similar)  
- Critical care foundation micro-grants (medical, surgical, transplant, neuro, cardiac)  

These are often **$5k–$50k**, perfect for hardware + scenario pack + small study.

## 2) Federal research (university route)

You (or a co-PI) need an **academic applicant organization**, usually with IRB/education research framing.

### NIH — digital health & AI tool evaluation
- NIH highlighted topic: **Digital Health and AI tools — validity and clinical utility**  
  https://grants.nih.gov/funding/find-a-fit-for-your-research/highlighted-topics/97  
- Fits: accuracy, safety, workflow impact, subgroup performance of CDS-like tools  
- Institutes to watch: **NLM** (informatics), **NIBIB** (devices/imaging/tech), **NHLBI**, **NIGMS** (training/sim sometimes), critical-care relevant ICs

### AHRQ — digital healthcare / patient safety / CDS
- Home of **clinical decision support** and digital healthcare research  
  https://digital.ahrq.gov/  
- Recurring themes: point-of-care digital interventions, AI + safety, workflow  
- Watch active PA/RFA pages (opportunities open/close; don’t trust stale PDFs)

### PCORI
- Better when you have **comparative effectiveness** or **methods for AI in CER**  
  https://www.pcori.org/funding-opportunities  
- Methods announcements have explicitly included **AI/ML methodology** tracks  
- Less ideal for pure hardware prototyping; better once you compare “cue on vs off” on patient-centered or clinician outcomes

### NSF
- **Smart Health**, CISE, education/cyberlearning angles  
- Sometimes better for **engineering + sensing + HCI** than pure clinical trials  
- Can pair with NIH via collaborative mechanisms

**Crisis Mirror angle that reviews well:**
- Cognitive forcing in high-stakes decompensation  
- Human-in-loop, advisory-only  
- Label loop (helpful/accurate) as a **safety science** contribution  
- Camera-free by design (privacy/ethics as a feature)

## 3) Small business commercialization (SBIR / STTR)

### NIH SBIR / STTR
- **Phase I:** ~up to ~$300k+ range (IC-dependent; check current omnibus)  
- **Phase II:** ~$1–2M+ range typical band  
- Portal: https://www.sbir.gov/ and IC-specific topics  
- NSF also has **Digital Health** SBIR topic:  
  https://seedfund.nsf.gov/topics/digital-health/

**Requirements in plain English:**
- A **US small business** (usually &lt;500 employees; PI employment rules differ SBIR vs STTR)  
- STTR **requires** a research institution partner (university) — often perfect if you keep clinical home + spin out company  
- Work must aim at a **product**, not only a paper

**Product framing for SBIR (safer):**
- “Simulation and training cognitive companion for critical care”  
- Optional later: “clinical workflow CDS software” with clear non-device claims until regulatory strategy exists

**Product framing that invites a tar pit too early:**
- “Detects patient deterioration and alerts the clinician to diagnose/treat”

## 4) ARPA-H (high upside, high miss rate)

- https://arpa-h.gov/explore-funding/open-funding-opportunities  
- Mission Office **ISOs** (open-ish calls) + named programs  
- Recent/related themes: trustworthy clinical AI, performance drift of CDS (e.g. PRECISE-AI lineage), agentic clinical tools in specific disease areas  

**Only chase if:**
- You can assemble a serious team (eng + clinical + eval + privacy)  
- You can stomach aggressive milestones  
- You are not using ARPA-H as “seed for a side project”

For Crisis Mirror today: **watch list, not week-1 application.**

## Regulatory / language = funding strategy

Reviewers and investors read your nouns.

| Say this | Avoid this (early) |
|----------|---------------------|
| Simulation cognitive companion | AI intensivist |
| Advisory protocol cue | Diagnoses deterioration |
| Education / QI pilot | Treats the patient |
| Human remains decision-maker | Autonomous closed-loop |
| Camera-free audio + HUD | Always-on video monitoring |
| Helpful/accurate label metrics | Guaranteed outcome improvement |

**Funding path splits on claims:**
1. **Education device / software** → societies, GME, some NSF/NIH education  
2. **CDS software study** → AHRQ/NIH digital health  
3. **Regulated medical device** → full FDA budget, different investors, slower  

Stay in **(1)→(2)** until you *choose* (3).

## What “good” looks like on a first grant aim page

**Aim 1** — Build camera-free sim companion (protocols + cue schema + label UI).  
**Aim 2** — Measure cue helpful/accurate, latency, and whether learners prefer on vs off without harm signals.  
**Aim 3** — Human factors: interruptibility, trust calibration, privacy acceptability (no camera).  

That’s fundable. “Replace intensivist judgment with glasses AI” is not.
