## Disclaimer (read first)

This material is **advisory regulatory analysis only**. It is **not legal advice**, not an IRB determination, and not a substitute for review by Virginia Commonwealth University’s Human Research Protection Program (VCU HRPP / IRB) or qualified counsel. **Final determination belongs to VCU HRPP / the VCU IRB.** Do not begin Phase 0.5 sessions until the applicable VCU determination or approval is in hand.

Nothing here authorizes contact with an IRB, use of VCU institutional systems, QI/QA content, or real patient data.

## Recommended path order

1. **PRIMARY — NHSR / not human research** via VIRBs + **HRP-503b**, framed as internal product go/no-go / quality-improvement–style evaluation of a prototype in sim (**not** generalizable research intended for publication). Obtain the **official HRPP/IRB NHSR letter BEFORE session 1** — do not self-determine casually.  
   https://research.vcu.edu/integrity-and-compliance/hrpp-irb/activities-requiring-irb-review/  
   https://research.vcu.edu/integrity-and-compliance/hrpp-irb/virbs/
2. **If plans change** (want to publish, or otherwise treat results as generalizable research): **STOP**. Obtain IRB review (exempt / expedited / full as applicable) **BEFORE any sessions that would be used for research**. You **cannot retrospectively publish** NHSR/internal sessions as research under the NHSR determination.
3. **Alternate only after re-determination:** exemption (45 CFR 46.104(d)(2)/(d)(3) + limited review) or expedited — see memo § “If plans change.”
