## 2. PRIMARY path for Phase 0.5: NHSR via HRP-503b

Public “Activities requiring IRB review” page:

- VCU IRB oversees activities that meet definitions of **research involving a human subject**.  
- **Investigators are responsible for determining whether an activity requires IRB review**, but may submit for an **official determination**.  
- If unclear, or if an official letter is needed, submit in VIRBs; decision issued in a letter to the PI.  
- For **Not Human Research (NHSR)** / non-engagement letters: complete and upload **HRP-503b TEMPLATE NHSR**.  
- IRB uses **HRP-310** (human research determination) and **HRP-311** (engagement).

https://research.vcu.edu/integrity-and-compliance/hrpp-irb/activities-requiring-irb-review/

**Phase 0.5 recommendation:** Do **not** self-determine casually. Request the **official NHSR letter via HRP-503b in VIRBs BEFORE session 1**. Public HRP-503b notes indicate the IRB will not make NHSR determinations **after** the activity has begun.

**Framing for the letter:** Internal product go/no-go / quality-improvement–style evaluation of a prototype in sim — **not** designed to contribute to generalizable knowledge; **no** publication of cue-test results as research.

Companion draft content: `phase0.5-nhsr-determination-request-DRAFT.md`.

## 3. Do you need IRB review? Who decides? (general)

**Exemption is different from NHSR:** On the review-levels page, VCU states that **even though research may be exempt, at VCU the determination of exemption must be made by the IRB.** Investigators do **not** self-finalize exemption.  
https://research.vcu.edu/integrity-and-compliance/hrpp-irb/types-of-irb-review/

Public Investigator Manual (**HRP-103**, toolkit): institution—not the investigator—determines whether Human Research is exempt; four classifications include Not Human Research, Exempt, Expedited, Convened IRB.  
Index: https://research.vcu.edu/integrity-and-compliance/hrpp-irb/hrpp-policies-and-guidance/  
Direct public file (as linked from that page): `https://research.vcu.edu/media/office-of-research-and-innovation/hrpptoolkit/HRP-103-INVESTIGATORMANUAL.docx`

OHRP (federal) also recommends against investigator self-exemption:  
https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/faq/exempt-research-determination/index.html

## 4. PI eligibility and training (applies to Dr. Kazior as PI; training also for Co-I Navarrete)

### 4.1 Who may be Principal Investigator

Public **HRP-103 Investigator Manual** (section “Who can serve as Principal Investigator…”):

- The PI must be classified by VCU as a **permanent, full-time or part-time employee of VCU or the VCUHS**, available to devote adequate time.  
- **Ineligible** as PI include: employees who are also students submitting student projects; **non-VCU employees (including affiliate faculty appointments)**; undergraduate/graduate students; post-doctoral students; fellows/residents; house staff; hourly/PRN staff; volunteers.

**Implication for this package:** Dr. Michael Kazior must meet the permanent VCU/VCUHS employee criterion to serve as PI. Dr. Sergio Navarrete as **Co-I** is fine for listing if eligible as study personnel, but **cannot** be PI if he falls in an ineligible category (e.g., non-VCU employee / affiliate-only).

- Getting started: before expedited/full board approval, designated COI Investigators must complete required disclosures; IRB approval waits for COI clearance.
