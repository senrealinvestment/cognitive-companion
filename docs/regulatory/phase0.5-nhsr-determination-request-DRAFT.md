## 3.0 Why this is not “research” (generalizable knowledge)

1. **Design purpose:** Decide whether Crisis Mirror should proceed to hardware purchase / later phases for **this product team** — a local administrative/product decision.  
2. **No publication intent:** Sergio’s authoritative decision: Phase 0.5 cue-test results will **not** be published as research. Internal notes, go/no-go memos, and design artifacts stay internal.  
3. **QI / product-evaluation analogy:** Collecting practical metrics to improve or green-light a local prototype in sim, not to establish scientific evidence for external audiences (OHRP QI FAQ framing).  
   https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/faq/quality-improvement-activities/index.html  
4. **Systematic ≠ research:** Scripted sessions and metrics make the work orderly; under **45 CFR 46.102(l)** the decisive element is design for **generalizable knowledge**, which this activity does not claim.

**OneTRAC (public) research vs QI:** https://onetrac.vcu.edu/research-vs-qi/
